## PricePilot:

AI-Powered Price & Restock Tracking Platform
Built with MERN, Playwright, Telegram API, and MongoDB.

## Flow of project:

User Registers
      ↓
Adds Product URL
      ↓
Stored in MongoDB
      ↓
Playwright Worker Runs
      ↓
Checks Product Price
      ↓
Updates Database
      ↓
Stores Price History
      ↓
Dashboard Updates
      ↓
Graph Generated
      ↓
Price Drops?
      ↓
YES
      ↓
Telegram Alert Sent
      ↓
User Buys Product
